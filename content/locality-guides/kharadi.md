## Location and borders

Kharadi is a suburb in the Eastern Metropolitan Corridor of Pune, on the banks of the Mula–Mutha river [1]. Wikipedia places Lohegaon to its north, the river with Magarpatta and Hadapsar to its south, Chandan Nagar, Wadgaon Sheri and Viman Nagar to its west, and Wagholi to its east [1]. It gives the drive to Pune Airport as about 15 minutes, and lists 411014 as the PIN code [1].

## How the area developed

Wikipedia describes Kharadi as originally a village. It says the IT boom that began around 2005 brought multinational companies, and that residential projects followed [1]. A 2011 feature in The Hindu BusinessLine gave similar timing, placing the start of the boom about five years earlier, and noted that the first expansion in eastern Pune had started at Chandan Nagar [2].

## Offices and employers

Wikipedia calls Kharadi a hub for information technology and business parks, lists a mix of telecom, banking, insurance and technology firms as having offices there, and mentions a World Trade Center campus of several buildings [1]. EON Free Zone, a commercial office development by Panchshil Realty, lists a Phase I and a Phase II [3]. When PMPML started a feeder bus from Ramwadi metro station in June 2024, its stops included Kharadi Chowk, World Trade Center Nos. 3 and 5, and Eon Gate No. 2 [4].

## Housing and everyday life

The 2011 BusinessLine feature said Kharadi had schools offering CBSE, IB and other streams, and that residents had connectivity to hospitals such as Ruby Hall Clinic, Jehangir and Inlaks & Budhrani, along with malls, multiplexes and food outlets [2]. It is dated, so check what is open today. Wikipedia says residential and office development was still moving at pace in the 2020s [1].

## Civic administration

Wikipedia names the Pune Municipal Corporation as the civic agency for Kharadi [1]. On traffic, the Pune City Police handle the area through its Chandan Nagar and Kharadi traffic divisions [5].

## Roads and traffic

Kharadi lies on the Pune–Nagar Road corridor towards Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar). In April 2025 the traffic police began an experimental set of route changes at Kharadi Bypass Chowk affecting that road, including keeping the Nagar Road-side lane at the chowk signal-free and sending vehicles bound for Mundhwa and Hadapsar through two lanes in the Chandan Nagar area [5]. The report called the changes experimental, so their present status should be checked [5]. The 2011 feature also pointed to easy access to the international airport [2].

## Metro status

In the reports checked, Metro did not yet reach Kharadi. The 6-km stretch from Ruby Hall Clinic to Ramwadi, on the Aqua Line, was inaugurated on 6 March 2024 [9], and on 5 September 2026 Punekar News noted that services still end at Ramwadi [8]. The Union Cabinet approved the Ramwadi–Wagholi/Vitthalwadi extension on 25 June 2025, naming Kharadi among the suburbs it would connect. The approval covered two elevated corridors, this one and Vanaz to Chandani Chowk, totalling 12.75 km with 13 stations, on a four-year completion schedule [6]. In July 2026 Maha Metro invited bids for four elevated stations on the first phase to Tuljabhavani Chowk [7]. In early September 2026 preparatory work began, with barricading near Ramwadi ahead of soil investigations. The first 4.7 km would have four stations, among them Kharadi Bypass [8]. None of the reports checked gave an opening date for the Kharadi stations.
