## Where Pimple Saudagar is

Pimple Saudagar is a neighbourhood in the city of Pimpri-Chinchwad and is administered by the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) [1]. Its postal code is 411027 [1]. Wikipedia's article on the neighbouring suburb of Rahatani places Rahatani between Pimple Saudagar and Kalewadi on the banks of the Pavana, about 7 km from Hinjawadi [3]. The Pimple Gurav article lists Pimple Saudagar among that neighbourhood's adjoining areas and mentions a public park in Pimple Gurav popularly known as Dinosaur Park [4].

## How the area developed

Pimple Saudagar was a village before it became part of the city. Wikipedia's account of PCMC says the corporation took its modern form on 11 October 1982, when surrounding villages including Pimple Saudagar, Sangvi, Rahatani, Thergaon, Pimple Gurav and Pimple Nilakh, along with parts of Wakad, were merged into it [2]. The Rahatani article describes that neighbouring suburb as a residential base for people working at the Rajiv Gandhi InfoTech Park in Hinjawadi [3].

## Housing and daily life

Housing in the area includes large apartment societies. A 2017 Times of India report on water supply quoted officials of several Pimple Saudagar societies, whose sizes ranged from a few hundred to about 1,000 flats. They said the municipal supply covered only part of their daily demand, and one chairman said the borewells ran dry from February, so tankers were needed until May [7]. Some societies had adopted rainwater harvesting to recharge groundwater [7]. That report is several years old, so current supply conditions should be checked locally.

Community life includes a periodic street-activity event: on Sunday 2 March 2025 a Happy Streets event, organised by two newspapers, was held on Jarwari Society Road between PK Chowk and Kunjir Chowk, with yoga, street cricket, chess and other activities from 6:30 am to 10 am [6]. In March 2025 more than a hundred residents formed a human chain in Pimple Saudagar to call for cleaner Pavana, Mula and Indrayani rivers and to oppose the riverfront development project [8].

## Roads and getting around

A two-lane bridge built by PCMC about 30 years before 2020 links Pimprigaon with Pimple Saudagar, and commuters use it to travel to places such as Pimpri Camp, Pimpri railway station and the PCMC main building. In February 2020 PCMC said it had finalised the design of a parallel bridge, with construction expected to start by 31 March 2020 [5]. The sources reviewed do not confirm its completion.

The Rahatani article says the Rainbow BRTS bus system has run in that suburb since November 2015 [3]. Pimpri-Chinchwad's BRTS corridors include the Aundh–Ravet route, and in August 2026 PCMC was reviewing its roughly 60 km network because of traffic and road-narrowing concerns [9]. On 22 May 2026 a flyover at Rakshak Chowk in Pimple Nilakh, another of the villages merged into PCMC in 1982, opened to traffic; officials expect it to improve connectivity between Aundh, Sangvi Phata, Ravet and Kiwale [10].

## Metro, with dates

The sources reviewed do not report a metro station open in Pimple Saudagar. PMRDA's Line 3 runs between Hinjewadi and Shivajinagar via Wakad, Balewadi and Baner. On 15 September 2026 Punekar News reported that 17 of its 23 stations were likely to open in a first phase by the end of September 2026, after the Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety had cleared the first 12 [11].

Separately, the proposed Bhakti Shakti–Wakad–Nashik Phata–Chakan corridor has moved through approvals. Maha-Metro submitted its detailed project report to PCMC in April 2026 [12], and on 5 September 2026 PCMC's General Body approved the report subject to conditions on land acquisition and traffic management [13]. A station list on Wikipedia, marked "DPR submitted", names Kokane Chowk, Vishal Nagar Corner and Pimple Saudagar on this line [14]. That list may predate the revised report, and the station locations in the revised report have not been confirmed in the sources reviewed, so no opening date or station location should be assumed.

## Civic administration

Pimple Saudagar lies within PCMC, a separate civic body from the Pune Municipal Corporation. PCMC was founded on 11 October 1982, is organised into eight administrative zones lettered A to H, and has its headquarters at PCMC Bhavan in Pimpri; its latest election was held on 15 January 2026 [2].
