## Where Kothrud is

Kothrud is a suburb in west-central Pune [1]. Wikipedia places the Vetal Tekdi hills on its north and Karve Nagar and the Mutha river on its south, and says it is centred on Paud Road and Karve Road [1]. Karve Road begins at Deccan and ends at Warje. Paud Road begins at Paud Phata and runs out to Paud through Chandani Chowk, which sits on the Katraj–Dehu bypass of the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway; the article gives that bypass as Kothrud's western boundary [1]. The postal PIN is 411038 [1]. In the Maratha period the area was known as Kothrud Baug, and the Mrutyunjayeshwar Temple there dates to the Peshwa era [1].

## How the area developed

Wikipedia describes Kothrud as one of Pune's earliest suburbs and a primarily residential one, which over the decades gained schools, colleges, hospitals, cinemas, malls, theatres and business establishments [1]. It reports that the wards under Kothrud recorded a population of 209,331 in the 2011 Census, according to PMC data, and that construction of residential buildings, mostly redevelopment of older buildings into taller ones, has been the main economic activity in recent years [1]. A March 2025 Times of India report on the area's liveability recorded residents' complaints about traffic, stalls encroaching on footpaths, potholed roads, garbage and crime. It quoted PMC's Kothrud-Bavdhan ward officer saying the civic body had launched a drive covering 30 identified hotspots, with solar-powered CCTV cameras planned at those locations [2].

## Work, study and daily life

The area's economy includes more than housing. The Wikipedia article mentions the Kothrud Industrial Estate of small and micro units and printing shops, a Cummins engine plant, the Automotive Research Association of India on the slopes of Vetal Tekdi, and the offices of some IT companies, and it says the suburb is conveniently connected to IT establishments run by Cognizant, Persistent Systems and Tech Mahindra [1]. The area also has many educational institutions: the article names MIT World Peace University, the Maharashtra Institute of Technology and MKSSS's Cummins College of Engineering for Women, and notes that the large student population supports a housing and food economy [1].

Everyday amenities named in the same article include local vegetable markets, State-organised weekly farmers' markets, walking trails up Vetal Tekdi, Joshi's Museum of Miniature Railways, the Vikram Pendse Cycle Museum and a PMC fire station on Karve Road near Kothrud Stand [1]. The article also says that groundwater is available through the year in most parts of Kothrud because of the geology and the aquifers recharged by the Vetal Tekdi hill complex [1].

## Roads and buses

PMPML runs the Kothrud Depot and Kothrud Stand, from which buses connect to Nigdi, Hadapsar, Swargate and Chakan [1]. Traffic is a recurring complaint. A PMC survey reported in May 2025 found 23 km of missing link roads in Kothrud and 8.92 km in the Karve Road area, roads shown in the development plan but not yet built [3].

## Metro, with dates

- **6 March 2022:** the first section of the Aqua Line, 5 km from Garware College to Vanaz with five stations, opened for revenue service [4]. Wikipedia lists Vanaz, Ideal Colony and Anand Nagar as the stations serving Kothrud [1].
- **25 June 2025:** the Union Cabinet approved Corridor 2A, Vanaz to Chandani Chowk, together with Corridor 2B towards Wagholi. The announcement says intercity buses from cities such as Mumbai and Bengaluru are planned to be integrated at Chandani Chowk [5].
- **6 February 2026:** the Free Press Journal reported that Pune Metro operated two routes, Vanaz–Ramwadi and Pimpri Chinchwad–Swargate, and that work had started on the extension from Vanaz to Chandni Chowk [8].
- **9 March 2026:** a joint venture won the contract for the Corridor 2A viaduct, with two stations named Kothrud Bus Depot and Chandani Chowk and a double-decker flyover at Kothrud Depot; the work was expected to take 27 months [6].
- **Reported 24 April 2026:** early-stage works, comprising soil testing and survey activities, were reported to be in progress in the Chandni Chowk area [7].

## Civic administration

Kothrud falls under the Pune Municipal Corporation, with a combined Kothrud-Bavdhan ward office [1][2].
