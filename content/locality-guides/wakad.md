## Location and borders

Wakad is a neighbourhood of Pimpri-Chinchwad on the north-western side of the Pune urban area [1]. Wikipedia places Pimple Nilakh, Pimple Saudagar and Aundh to its east, the Mula river with Baner, Pashan and Balewadi to its south, and Thergaon, Akurdi, Chinchwad and Ravet to its north [1]. The Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park lies in the adjoining Hinjewadi area [1].

## How the area developed

Wikipedia records that farmers on Wakad's black soil grew sugarcane, groundnut and onion, and that a temple of Mhatoba holds a yearly jatra [1]. Parts of Wakad were brought into the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation when it was reorganised in 1982, along with villages such as Sangvi, Rahatani, Thergaon and Pimple Saudagar [3]. By 2012 The Economic Times reported rising investment as roads, water lines and other civic infrastructure were developed. It listed the nearby Hinjewadi parks, the Mumbai–Bangalore bypass, the expressway to Mumbai and the Balewadi sports complex as working in the area's favour [2].

## Civic administration

Wakad comes under the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC), according to The Economic Times [2], and Wikipedia gives the same governing body [1]. PCMC is the civic body that governs the city of Pimpri-Chinchwad, and parts of Wakad were absorbed into it when it took its present form in 1982 [3]. Wikipedia describes its main responsibility as looking after the civic and infrastructural needs of its citizens [3].

## Employers and housing

Wikipedia says many residents work in technology companies at the Hinjewadi park, naming Infosys, Tata Technologies, Cognizant, TCS, Wipro, Tech Mahindra and Capgemini [1]. The 2012 Economic Times report said young IT professionals chose Wakad because the park was close. It put the area about 5 to 7 km from Aundh and Baner and about 20 km from Pune Airport [2]. That report was written from the viewpoint of flat buyers and developers, and it quoted one developer describing an apartment complex beside the Pune–Mumbai highway [2]. Wikipedia describes the neighbourhood as diverse and cosmopolitan [1].

## Roads and transport

Wakad Chowk, on the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway, is a boarding and drop-off point for intercity travellers, and hundreds of private buses, shared cabs and passenger vehicles use the junction every day [5]. In September 2025 traffic police trialled a one-way arrangement on the Wakad–Hinjawadi bridge during peak office hours; the report called it experimental, so its present status should be checked [4]. A November 2025 report said service roads on the highway between Wakad and Kiwale, a 9.7 km stretch, were under development [6].

## Metro

Wakad Chowk is the ninth of 23 stations on Pune Metro Line 3, and it falls within the first phase of 12 stations from Maan to R.K. Laxman Museum at Balewadi [9]. A 60-metre foot overbridge linking the station to the far side of the highway was installed on 29 June 2026 [5]. The safety regulator had permitted passenger operations on the first 12 stations, but as of 29 September 2026 services had not begun [10].

## Everyday life

Phoenix Mall of the Millennium stands in Wakad. Its developer described it as a 16-acre site with over 12 lakh square feet of leasable area [7], and India Retailing reported it open on 1 September 2023 [8]. Wikipedia names Indira College, Akshara International School and Euro School among the educational institutions in Wakad [1]. The 2012 Economic Times report noted that the Aditya Birla Memorial Hospital in Chinchwad is a few kilometres away [2].
