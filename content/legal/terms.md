- We do not guarantee that a contractor will contact you, give you a quotation, be available, or do the work well. Contractors are independent businesses, not our employees or agents.
- Before hiring anyone, we suggest you: ask for references and photos of past work, get a written quotation with scope and materials, agree a payment schedule tied to progress, avoid large advance payments, and keep receipts.
- "Get up to 3 free quotes" appears only where at least three active contractors accept requests for that job and area. A request is free for you; you never pay us for it.
