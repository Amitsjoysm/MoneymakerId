## 1. Who we are

MarketMind AI is run by **{{LEGAL_NAME}}** ("MarketMind AI", "we", "us"). We operate `marketmindai.com`, `food.marketmindai.com` and `construction.marketmindai.com` (together, the "Sites").

For the personal data described in this policy, we are the **Data Fiduciary** under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"): we decide why and how your data is used.

**Contact:** {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} · WhatsApp 91 98343 46179 · {{POSTAL_ADDRESS}}

## 2. The short version

- You can browse the Sites without giving us your name or phone number.
- We collect personal data mainly when **you** fill in one of our forms or message us.
- If you ask for construction quotes and **tick the consent box**, we share your request with **up to 3** matched contractors. We never sell your data.
- Our own visit measurement uses **no cookies** and does **not store your IP address**.
- We keep quote requests for **24 months**, then delete or anonymise them.
- You can withdraw consent, see, correct or delete your data, and complain, at any time (section 9).

## 3. The law we follow

We process personal data in line with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and the rules made under it, and other applicable Indian law. Where a rule is not yet in force, we follow it anyway.

## 4. What we collect, and why

### 4.1 Construction quote requests

| What we collect | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and mobile number | To contact you about your request on WhatsApp or phone, and (with your consent) to let up to 3 contractors contact you |
| Details of the job: service, locality, property type, whether you own or rent, when you want to start, area, quality tier, our cost estimate, and any notes you add | To understand the job, match suitable contractors and follow up |
| The date, time and version of the consent text you accepted | To prove your consent |
| How you found us: the page you started on, the type of page, the kind of site that referred you (search, social, AI assistant, direct), campaign tags in the link, and a short reference code | To learn which pages help people, and to recognise your request if you message us on WhatsApp |
| Follow-up information: whether we reached you, which contractors received your request, and (if you tell us) the quotes you received and the final price | To follow up properly, handle complaints, and publish **anonymised** local price ranges (never with your name or number) |

The consent box is **unticked** until you tick it. It reads: *"Share my details with up to 3 verified contractors and let MarketMind AI contact me on WhatsApp/phone about this request."*

### 4.2 "Remind me" sign-ups

If you ask us to remind you later (for example before the monsoon), we keep your name, mobile number, the service, your locality and the reminder date, only to send that reminder and follow up on it.

### 4.3 Business enquiries

When you ask about featured listings, advertising, sponsorships, claiming a business, or receiving customer enquiries as a contractor, we collect your name, phone, email, business name, category, locality, website, WhatsApp number, description and services, to reply to you and, if you become a customer, to provide the service.

### 4.4 Messages to us

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive your number or email address and what you write, and use them to reply. WhatsApp is operated by Meta under its own terms and privacy policy.

### 4.5 The Ask box

When you type a question into Ask, we store up to 200 characters of it and what we understood from it (dish or service, area, budget), without your IP address. To answer questions we have no data for, the question may be sent to our web-search and AI providers (section 6). **Please do not type personal details into Ask.**

### 4.6 Feedback

If you use "Was this useful?" or "Report outdated info", we store your answer and any note you add (up to 500 characters). Please do not include personal details in the note.

### 4.7 Visiting the Sites

- Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and browser information to deliver pages and protect the Sites from attacks and spam.
- To limit abuse and count distinct visitors, our servers compute a **keyed hash of your IP address** using a secret that changes every day. We store the hash, never the IP address itself, and the hash cannot be linked from one day to the next.
- Our own measurement counts anonymous interactions (for example "the calculator was used on this page"). It uses **no cookies**, and stores **no IP address and no browser identifier**. Counts are combined per day.
- **"Near me"**: if you allow it, your browser uses your location to sort results. Your location stays in your browser and is **never sent to us**.
- Analytics and advertising technologies are described in section 10 and in our Cookie Policy.

### 4.8 People who appear in our listings

Our listings describe businesses using public sources (such as a business's own website) and our own visits. Where a business is run by an individual, a listing may include that person's name and business contact details. We do not publish an individual's personal mobile number. You can ask us to correct or remove your details (section 9).

## 5. Sharing your quote request with contractors

- We share your request **only if you ticked the consent box**, and only with **up to 3** contractors who match your job and area, or with **one** contractor if they take the job exclusively. If you asked for a quote from a specific contractor, they are offered your request first.
- We first send a contractor a **summary without your phone number**. Your number is shared only after a contractor accepts your request.
- Contractors may pay us for receiving requests. This never changes how we rank them on our pages.
- Once a contractor has your details, they use them to contact you about your job. Please tell us if a contractor misuses your details: we will stop sending them requests.
- If you withdraw consent, we stop sharing your request immediately and ask contractors who already received it to stop using it.

| Provider | What they do for us |

We **do not sell** personal data. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our legal rights.

## 7. Where your data is processed

Our database is in India (Mumbai). Some providers above process data in other countries. We transfer personal data outside India only as Indian law permits.

## 8. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Quote requests, reminder sign-ups, and who they were shared with | **24 months** from the request, then we delete your name, number and notes and keep only anonymised job details (service, area, size, price range) |
| Requests marked as spam or tests | 30 days |
| Form submissions held temporarily while our database is unavailable | Until they reach the database (normally minutes); never more than 30 days |
| Our alert messages (Telegram and email) | As long as the request they relate to |
| Business enquiries and claims | 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer and the law requires us to keep business records longer |
| Ask questions | 12 months, then only the anonymous summary (dish or service, area) is kept |
| Feedback notes | 24 months |
| Encrypted backups | 30 days |

If you withdraw consent or ask us to delete your data, we act sooner (section 9), unless the law requires us to keep something.

## 9. Your rights, and how to use them

You can:

1. **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it: send "STOP" or "Withdraw" on WhatsApp to 91 98343 46179, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before it.
2. **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
3. **Correct, complete or update** data that is wrong or incomplete.
4. **Erase** your data when it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.
5. **Raise a grievance** with our Grievance Officer (section 11).
6. **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.
7. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to **complain to the Data Protection Board of India** under the DPDP Act.

**How we handle requests:** contact us from the same phone number or email address you used with us, so we can confirm it is you without asking for more data. We will acknowledge your request within **7 days** and complete it within **30 days**.

## 10. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Full details are in our **Cookie Policy**. In short:

- **Our own code sets no cookies.**
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts visits **without cookies**.
- **Google Analytics 4** measures how the Sites are used and can set cookies. We use Google's **Consent Mode**: for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies are not used unless you consent. You can opt out of Google Analytics on any site with [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that forms are submitted by people, not bots.

**Advertising (Google AdSense).** Some pages may show ads served by Google. When they do:

- **Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Sites or other websites.**
- **Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our Sites and/or other sites on the Internet.**
- **You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).** You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/).
- Learn [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.
- Ads are never shown on our quote forms, contact, legal or business pages.
