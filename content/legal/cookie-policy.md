## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser's local and session storage) used on `marketmindai.com`, `food.marketmindai.com` and `construction.marketmindai.com` (the "Sites"), run by **{{LEGAL_NAME}}**. It works together with our **Privacy Policy**.

A **cookie** is a small file a website asks your browser to store. **Local storage** and **session storage** are similar places in your browser; session storage is cleared when you close the tab.

## 2. The short version

- **Our own code sets no cookies.**
- Our own visit measurement is **cookieless** and stores **no IP address**.
- **Google Analytics** may set cookies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it does so only with your consent.
- **Google AdSense** ads, when shown, use cookies to serve and measure ads, which can include personalised ads.
- Our security provider, **Cloudflare**, may use strictly necessary technologies to protect the Sites and our forms.

## 3. What we and our providers use

### 3.1 Strictly necessary

These keep the Sites secure and the forms working. They cannot be switched off on our side.

| Who | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cloudflare (hosting and security) | May set security cookies to tell people from bots and protect the Sites, especially when it shows a security check. |
| Cloudflare Turnstile (form protection) | Runs a check in your browser when you use a form or Ask, to confirm a person is submitting it. |

### 3.2 Our own anonymous measurement (no cookies)

| What | Where it is kept | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A random ID for the current tab | Session storage (deleted when you close the tab) | Links the clicks in one visit together (for example "calculator used, then quote started"), without identifying you |
| Which version of a page layout you are seeing | Session storage | Lets us fairly test small layout changes |
| Small preferences you choose, such as closing the action bar (if stored) | Local storage | So the Sites remember your choice |

We do not store your IP address or browser identifier with these, and we count them only as daily totals.

### 3.3 Analytics

| Who | Cookies? | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare Web Analytics | **No cookies** | Counts page views and page speed. |
| Google Analytics 4 | **Yes**, set by Google | Measures how the Sites are used. We use Google's **Consent Mode**: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are not used unless you consent; elsewhere they are used by default. You can opt out on any site with [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). |

### 3.4 Advertising (only on pages that show ads)

Ads may appear on some food and guide pages. They **never** appear on our quote forms, contact, legal, business, contractor sign-up, Ask, thank-you or error pages.

When ads are shown by **Google AdSense**:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Sites or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our Sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/).
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message first. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.
- Learn [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 3.5 Links that take you elsewhere

Buttons such as **Send on WhatsApp**, **Directions**, **Call**, or **Order on Swiggy/Zomato** are plain links. Nothing from those services loads on our pages until you tap the link; after that, their own cookie and privacy policies apply.

### 3.6 Our private admin area

Our team's admin area (not open to the public) uses a login cookie set by Cloudflare Access after a one-time code is entered. It is used only by our team.

## 4. "Near me" and location

If you use "Near me", your browser asks your permission to share your location. The location is used **only in your browser** to sort results and is never sent to us or stored in a cookie.

## 5. Your choices

- **Browser settings:** you can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary technologies, forms and security checks may not work.
- **Google Analytics:** use [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Personalised ads:** use [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/).
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** you can give or refuse consent in the consent message, and change your choice later the same way.

## 6. Changes

We update this policy when the technologies we use change, and show the new date at the top.
