# MarketMind AI: Editorial Policy

*How we choose what to recommend, what our labels mean, how money does and does not affect what you see, and how to tell us when we are wrong.*

MarketMind AI helps people in Pune find good local food and understand what home construction and repair work should cost. Everything below applies to `marketmindai.com`, `food.marketmindai.com` and `construction.marketmindai.com`.

---

## 1. Our promises

1. **Evidence first.** Every fact we show (a price, opening hours, a dish, a service, a cost range) comes with its source and the date we last checked it. If we cannot back something up, we do not publish it.
2. **No fake anything.** We never invent businesses, prices, ratings, reviews, quotes or "only 2 slots left" scarcity.
3. **Money never buys a better organic rank.** Paid placements are always labelled and shown separately.
4. **People before machines.** We use AI to help sort and tidy information, never as the final word on a price, address or opening hours, and never to publish text a person has not reviewed.
5. **We fix mistakes quickly** and show when a page was last updated.

---

## 2. How we rank food recommendations

When you look at a page like "Best Biryani in Hinjewadi, Pune", each place gets a **food score** between 0 and 1. It is made of seven parts:

| Part | Share of the score | What it means in plain words |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevance | 20% | How well the place matches what you asked for: the dish, the area, the kind of meal. |
| Rating strength | 20% | Our own first-hand ratings, plus star ratings from sources that allow us to use them. When a place has only a few ratings, we pull its score towards the local average so that one lucky rating cannot put it on top. |
| Review strength | 15% | How many **independent** sources speak well of the place. We count positive mentions; we never copy review text. |
| Dish evidence | 15% | How much dated evidence there is that the place actually serves this dish. |
| Freshness | 10% | How recently its information was checked. Out-of-date facts count for less. |
| Value | 10% | How its checked prices compare with typical prices for the dish in the area. |
| Locality match | 10% | Whether it is really in, or right next to, the area you chose. |

A place with **no evidence that it serves the dish is left out**, however popular it is. When a part has no data, it scores zero rather than being guessed.

We only build a page like "Best Biryani in Hinjewadi" when there is enough real evidence: at least three places with fresh, checked evidence for that dish, and each one either visited by us or confirmed by at least two independent sources. If there is not enough evidence, the page does not exist yet. We would rather show nothing than pad a page.

## 3. How we rank contractors and service providers

Each provider gets a **provider score** between 0 and 1:

| Part | Share | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Service match | 25% | They do the specific job you need (for example terrace waterproofing, not just "waterproofing"). |
| Locality match | 15% | They work in your area. |
| Specialisation | 15% | Evidence that this kind of work is a focus for them. |
| Review strength | 15% | Positive mentions from independent sources. |
| Verification | 15% | Whether **we** have verified them, by checking their website, phone, business documents or a visit. |
| Freshness | 10% | How recently their information was checked. |
| Experience | 5% | Years in business, where there is evidence. |

## 4. What our labels mean

Labels are **calculated from data**, never assigned by hand or for money. Each label needs at least **three pieces of evidence** behind it; with fewer, no label is shown.

**How busy or talked-about a place is right now** (from a separate score made of recent mentions 25%, freshness 20%, demand on our site 20%, engagement 15% and quality 20%):

| Label | When it appears |
|---|---|
| Hot | score 0.80 or higher |
| Trending | 0.65 to 0.79 |
| Warm | 0.50 to 0.64 |
| (no label) | 0.35 to 0.49 |
| Cold | below 0.35 |

**Badges:**

| Badge | When it appears |
|---|---|
| Hidden gem | Quality in the top quarter of its area **and** visibility in the bottom quarter: good, but not yet widely known. |
| Best value | Value in the top fifth of its area, with at least two dated prices. |
| Late night | Opening hours past 11 pm, checked within the last 30 days. |
| Family / Office lunch | Tags from evidence or our own visits, and, for office lunch, distance to office clusters. |

**Trust labels:**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ Verified | **We** checked this business ourselves by website, phone, documents or a visit, on the date shown. It is never given automatically, and never because an AI found the business. |
| Recently checked | Information checked within the last 30 days. |
| Source-backed | At least one cited source supports the listing. |
| Price last checked / Hours last checked | The date of the newest evidence for that price or those hours. |

We do not use phrases like "#1 best in Pune", because "best" cannot be proven. We say **"our top recommendation based on current available evidence"** instead.

## 5. First-hand experiences

Some recommendations come from our own visits. A first-hand experience records the visit date, the dishes tried, the price we paid, a rating for each dish, notes, whether we would recommend it, and our own photos.

- We pay for our own meals and services. If a business ever gives us something free or discounted, the experience says so; otherwise we do not publish it.
- First-hand ratings appear as reviews only for our own visits. We never publish reviews written by or for a business.
- Our photos are our own. We never use photos from other websites or apps.

---

## 6. Paid placements, sponsorships, ads and affiliate links

We earn money from featured listings, sponsorships, contractor leads, display ads and, sometimes, affiliate links. Here is how we keep that separate from our recommendations:

- **Two separate scores.** Every listing has an **organic score** (Sections 2 and 3) and, separately, a commercial status. Payment can never change the organic score or the organic order.
- **Featured listings** appear in their own block, labelled **"Featured · paid placement"**, never mixed into the organic list. *Featured placement is paid.*
- **Sponsorships** (for example "Hinjewadi Food Guide · Sponsored by …") are labelled as sponsored and end on the date agreed.
- **Ads** are labelled **"Advertisement"** and never appear on quote forms, contact, legal, business, claim, contractor sign-up, Ask, thank-you or error pages.
- **Paid links** are marked `rel="sponsored"` for search engines.
- **Affiliate links:** *We may receive a commission from some links.* This never affects ranking.
- **"Get up to 3 free quotes"** appears only where at least three active contractors accept requests for that job and area. Otherwise we say "we'll connect you with a local contractor". A business that pays us is not implied to respond unless it has agreed to.
- **Founding-partner offers** ("first 5 providers in this area") are counted from real data. We never show fake scarcity.

## 7. Cost estimates are indicative

*Cost estimates are indicative and should not be treated as quotations.*

- Each cost range comes from a **cost model** for a service, scope, material and tier (budget, standard, premium). Every model cites **at least two independent sources** with dates, and is split into materials, labour, preparation, repair, transport and waste.
- Cost models are re-checked at least every **180 days**. Once a model passes its review date, the page says it is "being re-verified" and shows the date it was last reviewed, until it is checked again.
- Area estimates (for example wall area for a 2 BHK) show the assumptions we used, so you can adjust them.
- An area adjustment is applied only when sourced evidence, or at least three recent real quotes from that area, justify it. Real quote figures are shown only in anonymised, combined form ("₹X–₹Y from n jobs").
- Always get a written quote from a contractor after a site visit before you agree to any work.

## 8. How we use AI

- **What AI does:** tidies and classifies information (matching "chkn biryani" to chicken biryani, sorting a service into the right category), helps match ambiguous names, picks out whether a public mention is positive or negative, and drafts summaries for us to review. We use Groq for this. We use Exa to search the web and Firecrawl to read public pages.
- **What AI does not do:** it is **never** the source of truth for a price, an address or opening hours. Every such fact must be found word-for-word in a source, and our code re-checks the number or name against that source text before we accept it. If the AI and the source disagree, we reject the claim.
- **Reviewed before published:** text drafted with AI help is not shown on any page that search engines can index until a person has read it, checked it against its sources and marked it reviewed.
- **Live answers:** when our Ask box looks something up on the web in real time, those results are labelled **"found on the web just now"** and go through the same checks.
- **Never verified by AI:** a business is never marked ✓ Verified because an AI found it.

---

## 9. Sources we use, and sources we do not copy

- We use official business websites, menus, public articles, directories that allow it, our own visits, and information businesses give us.
- We **do not copy** articles, full reviews, other databases or photos. We take small, dated facts and cite where they came from.
- Platforms such as Zomato, Swiggy, Justdial, Google Maps and Sulekha help us **discover** that a business exists. We do not copy their content, and they never count as an independent source for our listings.

## 10. Corrections and "Report outdated info"

**Tell us:**
- Use **"Was this useful? / Report outdated info"** on any listing or recommendation list.
- Or message us on WhatsApp at **91 98343 46179**, or email **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.
- Businesses can also use **Claim your business** on our main site.

**What happens next:**
1. We look at every "outdated" report, aiming to do so **within 7 days**.
2. If a price, hours or other fact is wrong, we mark the old evidence as superseded, re-check the source (or visit), and publish the corrected fact with its new "last checked" date.
3. If we cannot confirm a fact any more, we remove it rather than leave it up.
4. The page's "Updated" date changes whenever its content changes.
5. Significant errors in a cost guide (a wrong range or a wrong method) are corrected and noted on the page.

We correct errors. We do not remove accurate, sourced information only because a business asks, and payment never buys a removal or a better position.
